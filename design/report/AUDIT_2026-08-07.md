# Skeptical full-chain audit — 2026-08-07 (pre-data-comparison)

Five independent adversarial reviews (S1 solver, Stage B digitizer, Stage C DREAM/DAQ,
certification/convention meta-review, Stage A C++) plus a dedicated investigation of the
undershoot mechanism (P6). Every finding below was verified against the code by the reviewer
(file:line given); the three headline mechanical claims (LUT window, noise scaling, ftst)
were independently re-confirmed before this report was written. No §9 target was fitted.

**Bottom line: the chain's units, statistics, and conventions are in much better shape than
the bug history suggested — the solver core survived a hand re-derivation, the e→fC→ADC
chain is exactly right, and the Polya/diffusion/resampling math all check out. But the audit
found one bias that points at the headline data tension, one mislabeled physics table, one
uncorrectable timing artifact in the decoded output, and several certifications that are
narrower than the plan's table implies. P6 (undershoot) is resolved and implemented.**

---

## A. Act before T14 (in order)

### A1. LUT time window truncates the late halo — points at the headline tension
`digitizer/kernel_lut.py:47` defaults `t_max_ns=1000`. The S1 product runs to 10 µs and the
DAQ area window is 1.92 µs. Far neighbours (d=±2/±3) are fed by sheet transport precisely on
the 0.5–>1 µs timescale (D ≈ 1 m²/s at 2 MΩ/sq), so the sim structurally cannot deliver
charge the real detector delivers between 1.0 and 1.92 µs — biasing the halo area shares
LOW, **the same direction as the "missing broad slow halo" §9 tension**. The ion/longitudinal
convolutions truncate at the same edge (the trailing-edge sibling of the fixed leading-edge
`apply_ion_transit` bug; `kernel_lut.py:214-221, 283`). `test_longitudinal.py`'s impulse sits
at t=0 so it is blind to this. → Rebuild the LUT with t_max ≥ ~3 µs and pad the longitudinal
convolutions; re-run the ρ_s scan table before concluding anything about the halo.

### A2. Sharing report's "in-gap" row is actually on-strip — no in-gap numbers exist
`solver/kernels.py:356-368` + `nearest_column` (:286-291) select the deposit by nearest pad
centre in ABSOLUTE x; from 15.6 mm every reachable pad centre is ~10 µm from a strip centre
(phase 790 µm under `ESL_PHASE_CENTERED_M` = on-strip). The §3 plan table's second row and
every product's `meta.sharing["in-gap"]` are therefore a second on-strip deposit differing
only in pad-ownership parity (corroborated: late X totals 0.366 vs 0.365, no gap-blocking
signature). Production G arrays are unaffected; `tau_g_reinterpretation.py` selects by an
explicit phase cut and its "∞ in-gap" conclusion stands. → Regenerate `sharing_report` with
phase-based selection (genuinely-in-gap pad centres exist at cols 10–21) and correct plan §3.

### A3. Decoded output: independent X/Y trigger phases, ftst written as zero
`digitizer/run.py:277-284` samples each view with a fresh uniform phase (`daq.py:107-108`);
`daq.py:173-174` writes ftst=0. Real data: one trigger, one phase, recorded in ftst (verified
nonzero/varying in a real det3 file); wft measures and corrects the X–Y offset from it
(`wft/calibrate.py:311-336`). The sim injects ~24.5 ns rms of fabricated inter-plane jitter
that is uncorrectable by construction. → One phase per event shared by both views; write
consistent ftst with data semantics. Required before T13b/T14 two-plane timing.

### A4. Real-pad kernel trust is anchored in a comment — assert it
The closed-form sum rule certifies only the fictitious tiling-pad geometry; production
0.68 mm-pad capture (0.665023) is checked against (0.68/0.78)²·0.875 = 0.6656 (0.08 %) only
in a comment (`test_charge_audit.py:118`), never asserted. T10's docstring ("same code path
as the sum rule") is wrong: `charge_budget_y/x` is separate indexing from
`sum_over_rows/columns` — T10 is two independently-written indexings agreeing (good, but
different claim). The charge audit reads its expectation from the product's own meta —
self-referential. → Add the one-line assert; fix the T10 docstring; note the trust hierarchy.
Related: the charge audit's +1.5 % residual is an EXCESS, which its own leak framings cannot
produce (they all predict deficits) — likely position-dependence of capture vs the grid-mean
reference; print the per-position spread and attribute it.

### A5. Wet-CF₄ certification certifies the dry column
§0a's "74.32 vs 74.7 µm/ns" row is dry Magboltz vs dry Magboltz. Whether the wet curves
bracket the measured 13–15 µm/ns at 233 V/cm — the check `wet_cf4_drift.py:122-124` itself
calls the one that matters, with "nothing downstream should be run" if it fails — is recorded
nowhere in this repo (JSON is EOS-only). → Pull the JSON, run the bracket check, record the
result. Precondition for the whole P1 path.

### A6. Attachment is absent from the chain — blocks the P1 gas switch
`digitize.py` transport has no survival term; `gas.py:53-56` reads only v/dL/dT (dry table
has no eta); `wet_cf4_drift.py:96-103` computes `eta_per_cm` that nothing consumes.
Negligible dry; NOT negligible for Ar/CF₄/iso + H₂O, and z-dependent (re-weights the depth
mixture behind the c1-vs-z diffusion signature). → Add e^(−∫eta dz) to transport when the
wet table lands.

### A7. Avalanche survival factor silently dropped from S3 calibration
Plan §5's "size cap bites" story is FALSE (no cap; the falling nev is the deliberate
per-voltage schedule in `mx17_aval_points.txt`). The real issue: `collect.py:38` fits the
Polya conditional on g>0, the raw per-slice `survival` field is discarded in the merge, and
`digitize.py:176` applies the conditional Polya to every mesh-surviving electron → per-
electron charge biased HIGH by 1/P(g>0), magnitude unknowable from the shipped calib (must
be recovered from EOS `avalanche/raw/`). Feeds the P2 saturation puzzle. → Recover survival
vs V from raw slices, carry it as a factor in transport (or fold into transparency).

### A8. FEU naming: sim `_07/_08` vs det3 FEUs 3/4
`run.py:374` vs its own comment (:221-229) and det3 `run_config.json`. The suffix IS the FEU
id in wft (`wft/io.py:39-41`) and keys the strip map; feus=[7,8] is a different physical
detector (mx17_2) in `qa_config.py`, and the det3 noise-source run is [3,4]. Fails loudly
(no files found) unless someone "fixes" it by renaming and silently inherits the wrong map.
→ Name output files by the target run's FEU ids.

## B. Resolved by this audit

### B1. P6 undershoot — mechanism found, implemented (`dream/shaper.py`)
The manual kills the old premise: SK damping ζ=0.75 is chosen "so that the global filter
response exhibits a 1 % only undershoot" — the biquad cannot give −4 to −6 %. The mechanism
is the CSA main pole: Rf·Cf = 5 µs (state1<31>=0 in `CosmicTb_MX17.cfg`; word order pinned
by the FEU 1 trigger override 0x881F/0xD043 = 50 µs + 283 ns), which the PZC exists to
cancel (§2.1.3) and evidently does not cancel fully. Implemented:
H(s) = [1 − β/(1+sτ_f)] · ω₀³/[(s+ω₀)(s²+1.5ω₀s+ω₀²)], τ_f = 5 µs, 5→100 % rise calibrated
to Table 9. Validation: β=0 reproduces the manual's 1 % (−1.12 %); β=0.75 gives −5.37 % on a
delta. Two independent run_71 features map to one β: undershoot depth −4..−6 % → β 0.6–0.9;
end-of-drift-ladder lobe −30 % at 700 V → β 0.85–0.9. β (PZC residual) is not in the
datasheet → scan/nuisance {0.5, 0.75, 1.0}, default 0.75, CLI `--pzc-residual`; the T14
undershoot target selects it, the ladder-lobe shape and late sag become cross-checks.
Legacy CR-RC² kept as `--filter-model crrc2` (its undershoot is exactly 0 — h ≥ 0).
Consequences now in the model: ~1–2 % peak loss (5 fC end-to-end check moves 102.4 → ~100.6
ADC at β=0.75), and ∫h = (1−β)∫h_nom, so long-window area budgets are no longer
shaper-invariant — the §7 area-trap paragraph's invariance argument now holds only at β=0.

### B2. The 98.0 vs 102.4 ADC discrepancy — explained, scale is right
`test_daq_wft.py:55-64` injects signal into every event and then uses the same events as
pedestal source → pedestal biased high by the injection riding on common mode. Reproduced:
clean pedestal events give 102.0/102.4. The derived 20.48 ADC/fC is correct. (The test's 10 %
tolerance would pass a +13 % gain error; tighten after separating pedestal events.)

### B3. P5 connector inversion — already resolved in the reference decoder
`Mx17StripMap.apply_orientation` reverses within n_channels=64, i.e. PER CONNECTOR,
unconditionally. Plan P5's "per-connector vs whole-FEU unresolved" is stale.

## C. Real but smaller (fix opportunistically)

- **Noise ~3.7 % loud** (`noise.py:186-189`): per-channel scale is σ_i/median(σ) but the
  average-spectrum trace has rms = √(mean variance) ≠ median σ (right-skewed). Exactly the
  3.5 % `selftest` residual. Fix: σ_i/√(Parseval(amp_chan)).
- **~1.3 % of avalanches booked one pad off in X** (`digitize.py:195` derives `col` from true
  x; `kernel_lut.py:80-88` band offsets from the nearest 40 µm sample; every second pad
  boundary splits them). One-line fix: derive col from the folded LUT sample. Sub-percent on
  means (alternating sign) but a genuine inconsistency both tests are blind to.
- **Packet mode Polya variance n× too large** (`digitize.py:166-176`: one single-electron
  draw scales the whole packet; correct is Gamma(n(1+θ))). Production packet=False unaffected
  — fix before profiling §12 item 11.
- **`charge_budget_x` does not fold y0** into the 1.56 mm box (`kernels.py:313`) — silent
  edge-clamp on absolute-y input; `charge_budget_y` folds/wraps. Make consistent.
- **`lut.ix()` clips at the superperiod seam** instead of wrapping (`kernel_lut.py:149-152`);
  also `ix()` itself is bypassed by T10's argmin — certify it or wrap it.
- **ny never converge-certified**: prompt kernel y-edge shoulder off by up to ~0.03 absolute
  at ny=512; converges at one doubling; ≤0.003 after 150 µm diffusion smear. One-off ny=1024
  re-solve (like the nt check) would close it.
- **Amp-gap/groove clusters** get full gain + mesh transparency + z-clip (`digitize.py:158`,
  contradicting `clusters.py:20-24`): ~0.5 % of clusters, ~9× overweight, prompt/unshared.
- **Fano correction (plan §7.1) exists in neither stage**; PAI region likewise. Means
  unbiased; resolution-type observables slightly narrow. Decide: implement or de-scope.
- **No truth block** in outputs (plan §7.7) and zero-electron events are dropped from decoded
  files (occupancy/eventId alignment vs real runs).
- **`gas.py:37` hard path** into `~/PycharmProjects/nTof_x17` — breaks condor portability.
- **Stale/latent**: `z_aval_um=5` default in the analytic ion model (measured 13.84);
  `spread.py:148 ptp()` (NumPy 2.x removal); `manifest --check` TypeError on a failed-sum-rule
  product; `_eig_cache` dead + ±ky blocks recomputed (free 2× in solves); Meta-tree branch
  addresses dangle after RunAction fill; ZS ZsChkSmp plan/code mismatch (code matches det3).

## F. Meta-observations

1. Every reviewer independently converged on the same weakness pattern: the certifications
   are strong where a closed form exists and thin exactly where production diverges from the
   closed-form geometry (real pads, off-lattice sources, late times, off-grid y). The fix is
   cheap in every case: one assert, one denser re-solve, probes drawn off-grid.
2. Four of the five reviews found at least one place where the PLAN's text and the code
   disagree (phantom avalanche cap, 3550 clip, V5 status, macro semantics). The plan is the
   authority document — these should be corrected there, not just here.
3. The two conventions that could silently corrupt T14 remain the x/y sign placeholder
   (`ActiveAreaFrame.hh:29-38` — flips checkerboard parity AND the 31.2 mm beat phase) and
   FEU naming (A8). Neither has a guard; the beat-phase observable is the only in-data check
   for the former.
