## 4. The gap axis — opened, quantified, and (pre-registered) unable to save both

Nothing pins det3's as-built amplification gap: only the nominal 150 µm
pillar gerber (the T6 map itself implies ~158 µm effective), in a production
series where det4's beam map shows continuously varying mesh height as the
real failure mode. Two independent Townsend-form calculations agree a ~135 µm
effective gap **closes the ×1.66 gain demand exactly** — and disagree only on
the slope's second-order response (α″ < 0 arm: slope falls to 0.26–0.29;
α-linear arm: stays 0.309 ± 0.010). **Both pre-registrations agree the gap
cannot reach the data's 0.449/10 V** — recorded, neither edited, in
**`GAP_SCAN_PREREG_2026-08-11.md`**; the scan (uniform-field arm, condor
11988753) adjudicates the curvature question as a by-product.

Two supporting results from the overnight fieldshape arm (landed 03:27 on
EOS): **mesh vs uniform slope 0.3106 vs 0.3107 per 10 V — the T6 field-map
shape is exonerated for the slope residual** — and the mesh map's gain factor
is flat in V (×0.579/0.557/0.561 across 460–520 V), which legitimises the
uniform arm for gain *ratios* (the caveat that the ×0.56 is measured at
150 µm only, with a signed near-field argument that the 135 µm factor sits
slightly further from 1, is in the prereg note).

Also caught before submission: `collect.py` grouping did not key on gap —
the two gap arms would have been silently merged (same class of failure as
the Penning-grouping near-miss). Fixed via `arm_tags()`.

## 5. The SPS data, interrogated — what it can and cannot say

Full analysis: **nTof_x17 `sps_beam_test_26/analysis/TWOGAS_HEADON_2026-08-11.md`**
(uniform event-wise estimators over six head-on arms, live band X 145–220 mm,
Y view, condor 11988756; pre-registration filed before results, one prediction
wrong and recorded).

What it CANNOT do — the reasons det4/SPS is not a T14 lever:
no Ar/iso 95/5 run exists at H4 (Ar/CO₂/iso 95/3/2 then Ar/CF₄/iso 88/10/2,
all wet ~1.5 %); det4 is the defective chamber; every ZS amplitude is
censored ("absolute gain not recoverable" is the campaign's own rule); one
RAW voltage.

What it DID say:

1. **The event-wise ±1 peak delay is +60 ns in all six arms** — two gases,
   ×2 in gain, drift 95→243 V/cm, two ZS thresholds and RAW. A resistive-layer
   constant, full stop.
2. **The shaped core rise is gas-invariant at matched censoring** (CO₂
   176–179 vs CF₄ 170 ns, 5 %, sign as the v-ratio predicts).
3. **ZS censoring sized**: −70 ns on rise, 0.42→0.35 on ±1 area, +7–8 %
   pseudo-tails where RAW shows the true −4…−9 % undershoot. The τ_s/c2
   "non-convergence" across runs was acceptance, not physics.
4. **The H4 water story closes**: the new wet-CO₂ bracket
   (`garfield_sim/results/drift_wetco2_*.json`, condor 11988752) puts the
   CO₂ epoch at 1.57 % H₂O — inside the CF₄ epoch's independent 1.3–1.7 % —
   and the cross-gas velocity ratio at 1.5 % predicts 1.13–1.20 vs measured
   1.14–1.17 (dry: 1.89). One flow-equilibrium water floor explains both
   gases' transport.

**Consequences for T14**: the gas-uncertainty excuse is dead for kernel-type
observables (invariant across a far larger gas change than any bench
contamination); and the old §9 ±1-delay tension (sim +93–142 ns vs measured
+54–61) can no longer be blamed on drift velocity — the measured delay is
transport-invariant, so the sim's over-prediction points at the Stage B
sharing chain, the same neighbourhood as the 18 % X/Y term.

## 6. What we are apparently not modeling correctly — the post-Prague list

In priority order, each with its designed test:

1. **α(E) shape (the slope residual, ×1.44).** Sole surviving suspect at
   fixed gas: Penning dead (outcome C), effective gap dead (both preregs),
   field-map shape dead (fieldshape A/B), gas axes dead (iso/water make it
   worse). The data's doubling-per-15.4 V is ordinary for published Ar/iso
   95/5 bulk-MM curves; the sim's per-22 V is the outlier. **Test:** run the
   identical Garfield pipeline as a 128 µm parallel-plate bulk MM against
   published measured gain curves — reproduces literature ⇒ det3-specific
   geometry; under-slopes literature ⇒ cross sections. Condor, days.
2. **The ion suppression ×3–4.7 (the rise residual).** Survives everything;
   remaining assumption surface after today: the rise-metric mapping is FIXED
   (defect 2), t0/frame is CLEARED, weighting-field family still shared.
   **Test:** a bench DREAM peaking-code scan (all 44 archived cfgs are code
   2 — no other-peaking data exists). f_ion ≈ 0.9 pins the shaped rise to the
   ion rectangle (weak peaking dependence); f_eff ≈ 0.2 makes rise track the
   shaper. Hours of bench time, decisive, pre-registerable.
3. **Stage B sharing (the ±1 delay and the 18 % X/Y term).** Now doubly
   implicated (§5). Instrument per-view charge budgets at the LUT level,
   per-FEU config into Stage C, drift-diffusion anisotropy.
4. **The absolute ADC scale.** 20.48 ADC/fC is a two-link datasheet chain
   whose only "certification" is a sim-internal round-trip; the FEU pulser
   has never been fired (`Feu_Pulser_Enable 0` in all 44 cfgs). A free ±10 %
   under every amplitude statement. **Test:** one pulser run on the bench.
5. **Known-mixture HV ladder** (Bronkhorst mixer logs since 2026-07-07):
   d lnG/dV against set isobutane fraction, directly. Kills the last gas
   caveat on the slope.
6. **q_sum is not an integral** — it is a lateral sum of per-strip temporal
   maxima; the "diffusion dilution closed by construction" ledger argument
   over-reaches. Re-close the row with an actual bound after the TRANSPFIX
   re-quote.
